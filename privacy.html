Privacy Policy

StarTide Journey works offline, without accounts, ads, tracking or third-party analytics. The app does not automatically send personal data, device identifiers or play records to the developer.

Up to ten play records, unfinished stages, stars, coins and settings stay on this device. Deleting the app removes its local data. System backups follow your device settings; there is no separate in-game cloud sync.

Motion, stage timing and audio-system status are processed on the device for gameplay and playback. The app does not record the microphone or collect your music.

If you email support, we use only the message and information you choose to send to answer your request. The app does not attach personal data or screenshots automatically.

This site remembers your language in your browser. It has no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and handles the connection information needed to serve it under GitHub’s privacy policy.
