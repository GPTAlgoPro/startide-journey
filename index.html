How to play

02

Level rules

    03

    Stars

    Every stage is timed. With a move target, three stars require both targets. You can finish after either target and replay for a better rating.

    10

    Play records

    Keep up to ten separate play records. Switch back to continue any run. Before an eleventh, you choose whether to replace the oldest. Progress and coins are separate; settings are shared.

    Boost next pour

    Spend 12 earned coins to boost the next pour. Material stock and clear rules stay unchanged.

    Shake sand

    Tilt to steer gravity. Shake or tap the phone icon to loosen material.
